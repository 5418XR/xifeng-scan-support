隐私政策

适用于西风文存 iPhone 版 1.0，自 2026 年 11 月 22 日起生效。

文字识别、页面整理和 PDF 生成均在设备上进行。App 无需登录。开发者不通过 App 收集照片、录像、识别文字、文档、身份信息或使用记录。App 不使用广告、分析 SDK 或跨应用追踪。

App 仅处理你在系统选择器中选取、主动分享或拍摄的资料。相机仅用于拍摄文档页面，你可以在 iPhone「设置」中关闭相机权限，继续通过相册或文件导入。云端素材可能先由 iCloud 或所选文件服务下载。

原图和存档保存在设备上，直到你删除。点「选择」删除存档后，可在「存档管理 → 最近删除」恢复或彻底删除。分享至待导入列表的副本可在那里单独删除。App 外已导出的文件和设备备份由你选用的文件服务及系统设置管理。

给支持邮箱发信由你主动发起。邮件服务会处理地址和你写入的内容，我们仅用于处理来信。你可以用同一邮箱请求删除支持邮件。请勿随信发送含敏感内容的原件。

使用帮助

把多张照片整理成 PDF

点「从相册选图」，轻点照片可选中或取消。要逐页拍摄，点「连续拍摄」，每页点一次「拍照」，拍齐后点「完成」。App 会整理并打开 PDF。

核对文字、保存与恢复

点阅读页底部的「存到文件」导出。右上角菜单可核对文字和查看原照片 PDF。返回存档库后按标题重开。误删可在右上角「存档管理 → 最近删除」恢复。

Privacy policy

Applies to Xifeng Scan 1.0 for iPhone, effective November 22, 2026.

Text recognition, page arrangement and PDF creation run on your device. No account is required. The developer does not collect photos, videos, recognized text, documents, identifiers or usage records through the app. The app has no advertising, analytics SDKs or cross-app tracking.

The app processes items you choose in a system picker, share with it, or photograph. Camera access is used to photograph document pages. You can turn it off in iPhone Settings and still import items from Photos or Files. iCloud or another file provider may download an item you choose.

Documents and their source images stay on the device until you delete them. Select a document in the library to move it to Recently Deleted. Open the library menu to restore it or delete it permanently. Shared items awaiting import can be deleted from the inbox. Exported copies and device backups are managed by the destination you select and your system settings.

If you email support, your email service handles your address and the content you send. We use it to respond to your request. You may request deletion of support correspondence at the same address. For privacy questions, email 3247028004@qq.com.
